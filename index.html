The story

A techie who found his real calling.

This isn't a career pivot out of desperation. It's a move toward something I'm genuinely better at — and more alive doing.

I started as a full-stack developer. Built products with React, Next.js, Node.js. Shipped real things that real people used. I was good at it.

But during my internship at MediaAMP, something shifted. I wasn't just writing code — I was participating in video shoots, drafting marketing copy, sitting in on GTM strategy sessions. And I noticed something: I was more energised in those rooms than in front of my IDE.

"The most interesting problem isn't how to build the product — it's how to make people care about it."

I started freelancing on the side. Writing social media scripts and captions for lifestyle brands. Doing video shoots. Building my own Instagram. One day I opened my analytics and saw 1 million accounts reached — in a single month. Without a single paid rupee.

That's when I knew. The pivot wasn't a pivot at all — it was arriving somewhere I was always headed. I bring a developer's rigour to every campaign: I understand funnels, data, user psychology, and how platforms actually work. But my instincts are a marketer's. I know what makes people stop scrolling. What makes them share. What makes a brand feel human.

That combination is rare. And I'm just getting started.
